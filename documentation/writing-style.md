# Writing Style Guide

Rules for all sales copy, FAQs, taglines, and product descriptions.

## NEVER Use

- **Em-dashes (—) or en-dashes (–)**: Split into separate sentences or use semicolons instead
- **AI slop**: Generic phrases like "committed to growth", "thriving network", "reach their potential", "genuine expertise", "transform your journey", "unlock your potential", "elevate your", "empower you"
- **Flowery language**: Excessive superlatives or corporate buzzwords

## DO Use

- **Short, direct sentences**
- **Semicolons** to connect related ideas: "Knowing what matters isn't optional; it's where everything starts."
- **Concrete, specific language** over vague promises

## Examples

**Bad:**

```
This transformative system will unlock your potential and empower you to reach new heights—giving you everything you need to thrive.
```

**Good:**

```
This system gives you clarity on your values, principles, and goals. Then it builds practical systems on that foundation.
```

**Bad:**

```
Join our thriving community of knowledge workers committed to continuous growth and excellence.
```

**Good:**

```
Join the community of knowledge workers.
```
